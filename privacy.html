Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

HEIC Photo Converter Pro is designed with privacy in mind. Photo conversion takes place locally on your device.

Photos

The app requests access to your photo library so you can select HEIC or HEIF images and save converted JPG or PNG images.

Your photos are processed locally on your device. HEIC Photo Converter Pro does not upload your photos to our servers.

In-App Purchases

HEIC Photo Converter Pro includes up to 25 free photo conversions. Users may purchase HEIC Converter Pro Lifetime as a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase to unlock unlimited conversions.

Purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. The app uses RevenueCat to help manage purchase status and determine whether the lifetime Pro entitlement is active.

RevenueCat may process technical information related to purchases and app usage that is necessary to provide purchase and entitlement functionality. Payment information is handled by Apple and is not provided to us.

Personal Information

We do not require you to create an account and we do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or the contents of your photos.

Analytics and Advertising

HEIC Photo Converter Pro does not contain third-party advertising. Purchase-related information may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat as necessary to provide and restore in-app purchases.

Data Storage

Conversion statistics and preferences may be stored locally on your device. Purchase entitlements are associated with your App Store purchase and are managed through Apple and RevenueCat.

Children's Privacy

HEIC Photo Converter Pro does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.
