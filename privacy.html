Privacy Policy for HEIC Converter Pro

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

HEIC Converter Pro is designed to protect your privacy. The app converts HEIC and HEIF photos to JPG or PNG directly on your device.

Information We Collect

HEIC Converter Pro does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit any personal information.

Photos and Media

The app may request access to your photo library so you can select HEIC or HEIF photos and save converted JPG or PNG copies. Your photos remain on your device. Photos are not uploaded to any server or sent to any third party.

On-Device Processing

All photo conversion happens locally on your device. HEIC Converter Pro does not use cloud processing, remote servers, analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

Photo Library Permission

Photo library permission is used only for:

  • Selecting photos to convert
  • Saving converted photos to your photo library
  • Creating or using the “HEIC Converted” album

Data Sharing

We do not share user data with third parties because we do not collect user data.

Children’s Privacy

HEIC Converter Pro does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
