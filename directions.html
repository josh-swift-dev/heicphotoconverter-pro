HEIC Photo Converter Pro Support

Thanks for using HEIC Photo Converter Pro.

HEIC Photo Converter Pro converts HEIC and HEIF photos into JPG or PNG images directly on your iPhone.

Free: Convert up to 25 photos.

Pro: Unlock unlimited conversions with a one-time lifetime in-app purchase. No subscription.

How to Convert Photos

Open HEIC Photo Converter Pro and select the HEIC or HEIF photos you want to convert. Choose JPG or PNG as the output format and tap the Convert button.

Converted photos are saved to your Photos library and may also appear in the HEIC Converted album.

Unlocking Pro

After using the 25 free photo conversions, you can unlock unlimited conversions by purchasing HEIC Converter Pro Lifetime.

This is a one-time purchase and is not a subscription.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased HEIC Converter Pro Lifetime, open the app's Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Make sure your device is signed in to the Apple Account associated with the original purchase.

Photos Aren't Appearing

HEIC Photo Converter Pro displays supported HEIC and HEIF photos. JPG, PNG, screenshots, and other unsupported formats may not appear in the HEIC/HEIF selection screen.

Privacy

Photo conversion occurs locally on your device. Your photos are not uploaded to our servers for conversion.
